# Contributing

## Report a question

The most useful contribution is a report of what you got in the exam.
[Open a report](https://github.com/flexilingo/tcf-canada-questions/issues/new?template=report-a-question.yml) and give the exam month, the
skill, the task and the question or topic in your own words (French or English).

Please do not include names, emails or any other personal information, and do not paste
copyrighted exam material: a paraphrase is what we need.

## What we do with reports

- We compare each report with the scenarios already in the bank. A new one is added; a known one
  raises its "Reported by" count.
- Every entry is reworded by FlexiLingo before it is published, so the repository never quotes
  anyone.
- The bank is regenerated from the FlexiLingo database, so please report through issues rather than
  editing the tables in a pull request.

By submitting a report you agree that it may be published under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE).
